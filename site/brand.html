Mark and wordmark

The mark is a keyhole on a rounded square, brass on the gold one and gold on the brass one. The keyhole also comes cut out (the background shows through it), black and white. On its own, it works as an icon and an avatar. The name is written in lowercase, always passforme, in Bricolage Grotesque.

Colours

Click a colour to copy its code. Gold glows on dark; brass is the same hue, darkened to read well on light.

How to use it

Do

  • Use the logo and the mark to talk about passforme or link to it
  • Pick gold or brass to suit the background
  • Scale it up or down, keeping its proportions

Avoid

  • Recolouring, stretching, rotating or adding shadows to the logo
  • Writing the name in capitals (PassForMe) or apart (pass for me)
  • Suggesting that a product of yours is an official passforme one
